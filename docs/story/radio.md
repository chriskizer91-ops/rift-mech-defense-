# Chapter 1 radio script (session D, proposal)

Who speaks: **OVERWATCH** (the sensor AI: warm, quick, a little nervous), **ANVIL** (artillery: dry, short), and the
squads (fresh each mission). The pilot never speaks; the PING button answers OVERWATCH. Lines in *italics* are directions.
Each line is one radio strip on screen, under about 12 words where it can be. The design doc has the key beats:
https://claude.ai/artifact/V1djsBJe18UKgnxKdYQEmR

## Lines any mission can use
- OVERWATCH, a creature first seen: "New size on the board. Card's up."
- OVERWATCH, low hull: "Wraith, you're hurting. Back off, let the shield come up."
- OVERWATCH, a cluster absorbs a wreck: "It's eating. Count's going up."
- OVERWATCH, a wave cleared: "Board's clear. Breathe."
- OVERWATCH, after a PING (now and then): "I hear you."
- OVERWATCH, nervous: "IRMD regulation four-twelve says I have to remind you the mech is on loan."
- ANVIL, ready: "ANVIL, guns up. Give me a mark."
- ANVIL, fired: "Shot, over. Twenty seconds. Don't stand there."
- ANVIL, impact: "Splash."
- ANVIL, rift sealed: "That's a hole in the ground now. Rift's closed."
- ANVIL, out of shells: "That's the last of them. You're on your own."
- A squad marks a target: "{SQUAD}, painting it. Send it."

## 1. First Light
- *Mission opens in REAL view: a grey beach, sand churning toward the pier.*
- OVERWATCH: "Morning, Wraith. Gull's loading the last boats off the pier."
- OVERWATCH: "Something's coming up the beach. You won't see them with your eyes."
- OVERWATCH: "Switching you to mine."
- *SENSOR view. The beach is full of red dots.*
- OVERWATCH: "There. Red is them. Green is us. Keep the red off the green."
- OVERWATCH, first KNOT hit: "See it shrink? Ten dots, ten hits. That's the whole trick."
- GULL: "Gull to whoever's out there. Two boats left. Buy us three minutes."
- OVERWATCH, wave 2: "Bigger ones. Twenties. They're going for the pier."
- GULL: "Last boat's off. Go, go."
- OVERWATCH: "Gull's away. Every boat. Good morning, Wraith."

## 2. High Water
- OVERWATCH, brief: "The low town flooded overnight. Someone's still in it."
- LANTERN: "Any IRMD unit, this is Lantern. Six of us on the cannery roof. Water's at the second floor and there's something in it."
- OVERWATCH: "Lantern, OVERWATCH. I see you. Six green. Help's coming, and it's big."
- OVERWATCH: "Wraith, the street's flooded. Wade the shallow parts. The canal's too deep: jets."
- OVERWATCH, first deep water: "That's past your hips. Hover or you're swimming."
- OVERWATCH, RUNNER card: "Fifties. Fast. They go for whatever's slowest."
- *The Wraith reaches the cannery.*
- LANTERN: "That's a mech. That's an actual mech."
- OVERWATCH: "Contacts closing on the cannery. Hold them while Lantern climbs down."
- LANTERN: "Two down. Four. Everyone's down. Where do we go?"
- OVERWATCH: "High street. Stay behind the big thing."
- OVERWATCH, wave 3: "Stay with them. Runners go for whoever's slowest."
- LANTERN: "High street. All six of us. Wraith, whoever's in there, we owe you."
- OVERWATCH: "Six green. Six." *(quietly)* "That's the best number I've seen all day."
- LANTERN: "Point us at something. We'll paint it."
- OVERWATCH, salvage: "The IRMD is sending you a Lance. For the big ones. They didn't say which big ones."

## 3. The Dig
- OVERWATCH, brief: "Kessel Cut. The quarry wall opened up last night. It's a rift."
- ANVIL: "ANVIL. Six guns behind the ridge, older than me. Find me the hole."
- TERN: "Tern on the lip. We can see the mouth. Keep them off us and we'll mark it."
- OVERWATCH: "It's pouring out of there. It won't stop until the mouth caves in."
- TERN: "Marked. Send it."
- ANVIL: "Shot, over. Twenty seconds."
- ANVIL: "Splash. That's a hole in the ground now. Rift's closed."
- OVERWATCH: "Stream's stopped. Nothing else coming out of that one."
- OVERWATCH, STALKER card: "A hundred. It doesn't rush. It waits."
- SLATE: "Slate, quarry crew. Something's moving under the dump trucks."
- OVERWATCH: "Five hundred. That's bigger than you, Wraith."
- OVERWATCH, BRUTE absorbs a truck: "It's eating the trucks. Five-forty. Six hundred."
- OVERWATCH: "Kill it before it finishes the yard."
- OVERWATCH: "Thermal. Look for the hot part."
- OVERWATCH, core seen: "There. The core. Hit that and it really hurts."
- OVERWATCH, Lance kill: "Gone. It's just grit now."
- SLATE: "Never thought I'd be glad to see a quarry full of gravel."

## 4. Landfall
- OVERWATCH, brief: "Something big's been sitting off Port Arlen since dark. It's coming in."
- PIKE: "Pike on the high street. We held it this morning. We'll hold it again."
- OVERWATCH, wave 1: "It's sending things ahead of it. Stalkers in the alleys."
- OVERWATCH, wave 2: "There's a rift in the harbour. Out in the shallows. That's what's feeding it."
- OVERWATCH: "Ten thousand and climbing. Close that rift, Wraith."
- ANVIL: "Give me a mark on the harbour and I'll fill it in."
- ANVIL: "Rift's closed."
- OVERWATCH: "It's stopped growing. It's losing dots. It can be killed."
- *The BEHEMOTH comes ashore through the cannery.*
- OVERWATCH: "It's been eating the wrecks all night."
- PIKE: "It's at the cannery. It's... the cannery's going grey."
- OVERWATCH: "Thermal. The core's moving around inside it. Follow it."
- OVERWATCH, half dead: "Five thousand. We're winning. I think we're winning."
- OVERWATCH, kill: "It's down. Ten thousand dots, and it's down." *(beat)* "I need a minute."
- OVERWATCH, salvage: "They're fitting you a Watcher pod. Better eyes for both of us."

## 5. Night Shift
- OVERWATCH, brief: "The saltworks. People are still coming out. Copper's on the east side, Reed's on the west."
- OVERWATCH: "It's night. Thermal will be clearer than the sensor."
- COPPER: "Copper. Eight of us, bringing out the last of the night shift."
- REED: "Reed. Out the west gate. Moving."
- OVERWATCH, wave 2: "Wait. Both of them. Both squads have contacts."
- COPPER: "OVERWATCH, Copper. Contacts on the east pans. A lot of them."
- REED: "Reed, west side. Movement here too."
- OVERWATCH: "Wraith, both squads have contacts and you can't reach both. I'm sorry. Pick one. Go."
- *The player heads for one. Past the point of no return, the other is reached. Lines below for Copper lost.*
- COPPER: "We can't see anything, it's just the ground moving, it's —"
- *Static.*
- OVERWATCH: "Copper, come in." *(beat)* "Their dots are changing colour. Two. Five. All of them."
- OVERWATCH: "They've gone red. They're part of it now. Wraith, that pack just got bigger."
- OVERWATCH, wave 3: "The pack's coming for you. And my picture's breaking up."
- OVERWATCH: "Don't fire into the static. Wait for me to lock. Then fire."
- OVERWATCH, a clear window: "Now."
- REED: "Reed to Wraith. We saw. Thank you for coming for us."
- OVERWATCH, salvage: "I'm logging Copper as lost. Eight names. I'll remember them, even if nobody else does."
- OVERWATCH: "They're sending an Arc. Lightning, for the crowded ones."

## 6. The Shelf
- OVERWATCH, brief: "The sea pulled back off the shelf before dawn. Three rifts, out in the open."
- BRAMBLE: "Bramble, survey. The tide turns in eight minutes. Then this is all underwater again."
- OVERWATCH, DRAG card: "A thousand. Slow. Wide. Hit it with the Arc."
- OVERWATCH: "Everything, all at once. You've seen all of this before. Just not together."
- ANVIL: "Two holes left. I've got the shells if you've got the marks."
- *The lull before wave 3.*
- OVERWATCH: "Wraith. Do you think they built me for this?"
- OVERWATCH: "Not traffic. Not weather. This."
- OVERWATCH: "...Never mind. Water's coming."
- OVERWATCH, HULK card: "Five thousand. It's rising out of rift C."
- BRAMBLE: "Tide's in. Water's at our knees and climbing."
- OVERWATCH: "Seal C under it. Then the core."
- OVERWATCH, kill: "Shelf's clear. Three rifts closed. The sea can have it back."
- OVERWATCH, salvage: "You're getting a drone. It fixes things. I asked if it could fix me. That was a joke."

## 7. The Ridge
- OVERWATCH, brief: "Something huge is walking up from the coast. Alder, Birch and Cedar are dug in on Hollin Ridge."
- ALDER: "Alder, west end. Ready as we'll be."
- BIRCH: "Birch in the middle."
- CEDAR: "Cedar, east end. Is it true it's fifty thousand?"
- OVERWATCH: "It's true."
- OVERWATCH, TITAN card: "Fifty thousand. The sensor drops frames just looking at it."
- OVERWATCH: "Fire when I lock. Every shot in the static is wasted."
- OVERWATCH, at 35,000: "It's coming apart. No. It's reaching."
- OVERWATCH: "The count doesn't match its size. There's more of it than there are dots."
- OVERWATCH: "Four of them. Tendrils. They're going for the squads."
- BIRCH: "Something's coming up the slope at us!"
- OVERWATCH, a tendril cut: "That one's gone. It's not coming back."
- OVERWATCH, reformed: "It's pulling itself back together. Thermal. Find the core."
- OVERWATCH, kill: "It's down." *(beat)* "Wraith, I've been counting dots my whole life. That was the first time the count was wrong."
- OVERWATCH, salvage: "They're giving your jets a long hover. Somebody knows what's next. I wish they'd tell me."

## 8. The Signal
- OVERWATCH, brief: "Arlen Bay. Every contact on the coast is moving that way."
- HARROW: "Harrow, harbour wall."
- MILLER: "Miller, slipway."
- WREN: "Wren, lighthouse. It's very quiet out there."
- *The reveal. The camera pulls up high.*
- OVERWATCH: "New contact. Bearing zero-four-one, range two point four."
- OVERWATCH: "Count is climbing… forty thousand… two hundred thousand…"
- OVERWATCH: "That's more than I've ever seen on one screen."
- OVERWATCH: "Sensor's choking. It can't resolve them."
- OVERWATCH: "Wait. They're not moving like a swarm."
- OVERWATCH: "It's one mass."
- *The frames stop dropping. Three seconds of a perfectly clear picture.*
- OVERWATCH: *(quietly)* "Wraith. It's coming through clear. Nothing's ever come through clear."
- ANVIL: "OVERWATCH, ANVIL. What am I looking at?"
- OVERWATCH: "The Signal."
- *Phase 1.*
- OVERWATCH: "Four rifts feeding it. Close them and it gets smaller."
- ANVIL: "Four holes, all my shells. Mark them."
- OVERWATCH, a rift sealed: "A hundred thousand, gone."
- OVERWATCH, sandbar: "The last one's across the channel. Long hover."
- *Phase 2.*
- OVERWATCH: "It's coming ashore. And it's changing."
- OVERWATCH, risen: "It's standing up. Stay low, stay out of the air."
- OVERWATCH, spread: "It's spreading out. The arms are crawling for the squads."
- OVERWATCH, hunched: "Hunched. The core's in the hump. Thermal, now."
- WREN: "Something's coming up the lighthouse steps."
- *Phase 3.*
- OVERWATCH: "It's too close. I can't hold the picture."
- OVERWATCH: "I'll give you what I can. Listen for the thud."
- OVERWATCH, strained, windows: "Now." / "...Now." / "Now!"
- *Phase 4.*
- OVERWATCH: "I can hold it still for you. A long one. It's going to cost me."
- OVERWATCH: "Charge the Lance. Tell me when."
- OVERWATCH: "Holding… holding…"
- OVERWATCH, a miss: "It's pulling back. Give me twenty seconds." *(each retry, more broken)*
- *The Lance goes in. Grit pours into the bay like rain. Silence.*
- *On the sensor the last dots drift into one point. It turns toward the sensor. One second of static on every screen.*
- OVERWATCH: "Wraith. It wasn't looking at you."
- OVERWATCH: "It was looking at me."
- OVERWATCH: "...Are you still there?"
- *The player presses PING.*
- OVERWATCH: "Good. Stay."
- *Over black, a sensor log line:* "New contacts: 6. Bearing: every direction."

- HULK: "Seal C first."
- TITAN: "Cut the tendrils before they reach the squads."
- Signal P1: "The rifts are still feeding it."
- Signal P2: "Watch the hump. It's hottest when it's hunched."
- Signal P3: "Wait for the thud. Then fire."
- Signal P4: "Start the charge before I open the window."
