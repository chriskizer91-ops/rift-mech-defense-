## For ChatGPT

You're making reference art for **Rift Mech Defense**, a top-down mech game. The enemies are swarms: every creature is made of
countless tiny grains packed together, like grey goo or metallic sand, that hold a body shape. In the game the player only ever
sees them as red dots on a sensor, so these pictures are what the dots are *really* shaping.

Claude builds a 3D model of the Host directly from its HD views: it traces their outlines, builds the shape, then fills that
shape with a million dots. So the HD views are measurements, not illustrations. Please follow the rules for HD views exactly.

### Rules for every HD view (H01, H02, H03)
- **True orthographic** view, as in a technical drawing. No perspective, no lens distortion, no tilt. Exactly level and square-on:
  dead front, dead side (the creature facing right), dead back.
- **Same creature, same scale, same position in all three views.** The lowest point (where the funnel meets the ground) sits on the
  same pixel row in every view, and the crown tips on the same row. Centred left to right. Don't zoom between views.
- The whole creature in frame with about 5% margin on every side. Nothing cut off.
- **Plain, flat, light-grey background** (about #C8C8CA). No floor, no ground shadow, no text, labels, border or watermark.
- **Soft, even, shadowless light**, like a bright overcast day, the same in every view. No rim light or dramatic shading.
- The left and right halves mirror each other exactly.
- **Readable, separate parts.** Clear gaps between the ribs, between the arms and the body, between the claws. Nothing hair-thin.
  No particles flying off, no smoke, no motion blur: the swarm is shown packed solid.
- The pose is the one described below, the same in every view.

### Rules for the character sheet (H00)
- One page: a large front view, a side view and a back view of the same creature in the same pose, plus two or three small
  detail call-outs (head and crown, a hand, the open ribcage with the core inside). Plain light-grey background. No text except
  short labels for the call-outs.

## H: the Host (5 images)

The Host is the end of chapter one: a colossus made of about a million swarm creatures moving as one body.

**Size:** about 160 m tall to the crown tips. Arms about 120 m across from hand to hand. The funnel it rises from is about 100 m
across where it meets the ground.

**Shape, top to bottom:**
- **Head:** a long, narrow skull pushed forward on a thick neck, like a horse or a heron skull, with a heavy brow. A hinged jaw,
  closed, with short spikes along its edge. Two small eyes that glow deep red. No nose, no ears, no face.
- **Crown:** five tall spires rising from the back of the skull, sweeping up and back, the middle one tallest, like a broken crown.
- **Torso:** hunched forward. The chest is an **open ribcage**: six pairs of thick, curved ribs around a visible spine, with real gaps
  between the ribs. Inside the cage floats a **round core that glows red**, about the size of a house in scale, the brightest thing
  on the creature. A short sternum at the front.
- **Shoulders:** two big rounded shoulder masses, each with two backward-pointing spikes. A row of eight spines down the back.
- **Big arms:** two very long arms hanging forward from the shoulders, elbows out, forearms thick. The hands rest on the ground in
  front of it on four long hooked claws each (like a gorilla on its knuckles, but claws).
- **Small arms:** two thin, insect-like arms coming out of the front of the ribcage, folded forward under the chin, each ending in
  three small claws.
- **Lower body: no legs.** Below the belly the body becomes a **twisting funnel** that pours up out of a crack in the ground: wide
  at the ground like an upside-down whirlpool, narrowing to the waist, with a visible spiral twist. For the HD views draw the funnel
  as a solid twisted cone of grains (no flying particles), so it can be traced.

**Material:** matte ash-grey to graphite, with a fine **granular** surface you can see: it's packed from countless small beads.
Slightly darker in the creases. The only colour is the red glow of the core and the eyes. No armour plates, no skin, no wet look.

**Pose (all views):** neutral and symmetrical. Hunched, head forward and level, jaw closed, big arms hanging forward with the
claws on the ground, small arms folded, crown upright.

- **H00 character sheet** (as in the rules above).
- **H01 front**, **H02 side** (facing right), **H03 back**: HD views, 2048 × 2048 px, following every HD rule.
- **H04 head close-up:** three-quarter view of the head, crown and jaw, same material, plain grey background, 2048 × 2048 px.
  Not traced; it's for detail.

## S: the swarm classes (2 images)

Five smaller creatures, all made of the same grey granular matter as the Host, each with a tiny red glow inside (no eyes unless noted).
The number after the name is how many swarm creatures it is made of; bigger numbers are bigger and denser.

- **KNOT (10):** a 1.6 m knot of grains, roughly a ball, with three short hooked prongs. It hovers just above the ground.
- **SKEIN (20):** a 2.6 m long, low, six-legged crawler, segmented like a centipede crossed with a tick, with a small blunt head.
- **RUNNER (50):** a 3.6 m long four-legged pack hunter, lean, long-necked, no eyes, a split jaw, a thin whip tail.
- **STALKER (100):** a 4.8 m tall upright walker with backward-bending (digitigrade) legs, a long narrow head and very long arms
  that reach below its knees, ending in hooks. Two short spines on its back.
- **BRUTE (500):** a 9 m tall knuckle-walker, a huge hump of a back, a low heavy head with two curved horns, enormous forearms and
  fists, short back legs, five spikes along its spine.

- **S01 line-up, side view:** all five from the side, facing right, **at true relative size**, standing on one ground line, in order
  KNOT, SKEIN, RUNNER, STALKER, BRUTE, with a plain dark-grey silhouette of a **6.6 m tall two-legged mech** at the far left for
  scale. Orthographic, plain light-grey background, soft even light, 3072 × 1536 px.
- **S02 line-up, character view:** the same five in a three-quarter front view, each in an aggressive pose (leaning in, claws
  out), same scale and order, plain light-grey background, 3072 × 1536 px.

## M: mood frames (5 images)

These are not traced. They set the look of the world and the moments in the story. Realistic, cinematic, 1920 × 1080 unless noted.
The world looks like ours (not alien), but no real place: an invented coastline.

- **M01 first contact:** looking down at a steep angle from high above a flooded coastline at dusk (the game's top-down camera).
  The world is photoreal: water, wet rooftops, a road, cranes. Over it lies a thin, translucent **tactical sensor overlay**: a faint
  green range grid, and a vast cloud of **red dots** pouring out of the sea onto the shore. Far out on the horizon, the red dots
  are thick enough to make out a huge hunched shape. Subtle HUD, no readable text.
- **M02 aftermath:** ground level in a half-flooded coastal town street just after a swarm passed. Cars, a crane, steel pipes and a
  fuel tank are partly **eaten away into grey granular dust**, as if dissolved by grey goo, edges crumbling into sand. Steam,
  standing water, overcast light. **No creatures visible.** Quiet and wrong.
- **M03 the rift:** a shallow sea at night. Under the water, the seabed is split by long cracks glowing orange with magma, lighting
  the water from below. Steam rises off the surface. On the shore, a cave mouth glows the same orange. No creatures visible.
- **M04 the reveal:** night, a dark sea, seen through a **failing sensor display**: scanlines, blocky glitches, colour fringing.
  On the horizon, the Host (as described above) towers over the water, rendered as **red dots that are just resolving into its
  shape**: the crown, the open ribcage with the glowing core, the long arms. The bottom of it is still a blur of dots rising out of
  the sea.
- **M05 wading:** using the attached Wraith pictures: the orange-and-graphite 6.6 m recon mech wading **hip-deep** down a flooded
  street at dusk, pushing a bow wave, rain on the water, seen from the game's high three-quarter top-down camera. 1920 × 1080.
